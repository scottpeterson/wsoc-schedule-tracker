What happened

North Central (IL), ranked 13th, was scheduled to play at UChicago, ranked 21st, on Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 4 PM Central on the grass field at Stagg Field. UChicago had announced on September 22 that the game would stream on the FloSports Channel through Prime Video and on FloCollege, its first nationally distributed broadcast and the first University Athletic Association game on Prime Video. Both teams were unbeaten at the time of that announcement. UChicago leads the series 9-0-1 and won 2-1 in Naperville in 2025.

It rained in Chicago through the day. At 10:42 AM Central, North Central announced the game was postponed because of weather. At 11:01 AM, UChicago published an article saying the North Central coaching staff had decided not to travel, that UChicago had prepared both the grass field and the artificial turf, and that the game would be recorded as a forfeit win for UChicago. North Central has not commented further. The NCAA's scoreboard lists the game as canceled.
